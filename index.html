Gentry GMT is a UK-based independent dealer in luxury Swiss timepieces and fine jewellery, operating since 2017. We offer a discreet, personal service to every client — whether you are looking to buy, sell, or trade a cherished piece.

Every item we handle is carefully authenticated before it reaches you — reflected in over 3,000 verified transactions and a 100% positive feedback record.

All purchases are dispatched via tracked and insured next-day delivery — so your piece arrives safely and exactly as described.
