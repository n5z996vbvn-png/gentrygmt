Gentry GMT is a UK-based independent dealer — so every transaction is simple, fully protected, and free from import duties or hidden charges. We have been operating since 2017, offering a discreet and personal service to every client.

Every item we handle is carefully authenticated before it reaches you. We take pride in the trust our clients place in us — a trust reflected in over 3,000 verified transactions and a 100% positive feedback record.

All purchases are dispatched via tracked and insured next-day delivery — so your piece arrives safely, swiftly, and exactly as described.

A selection of pieces are handled privately and made available outside of our public listings — enquire directly to learn more.
